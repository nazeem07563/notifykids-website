Reliable delivery
10-layer smart sending engine

We've spent months building intelligent delivery technology so your messages always reach parents — safely and consistently, every single school day.

1
Gradual warmup schedule
New numbers send fewer messages at first and increase gradually — so WhatsApp recognises your school as a trusted, established sender.
2
Respectful sending hours
Messages only go out between 7am and 9pm. No late-night sends — parents appreciate it, and it keeps your school's number in good standing.
3
1,600+ message variations
40 warm openings × 40 warm closings — every parent receives a slightly different, personal message. Never a copy-paste broadcast.
4
Intelligent message pacing
4–14 second gaps between each message, with natural longer pauses every 25 sends — mimics real human conversation, not a bulk blast.
5
Automatic circuit breaker
If delivery starts failing, the system pauses automatically and alerts your school on WhatsApp — protecting your number before any damage is done.
6
Invalid number detection
Numbers that aren't on WhatsApp are automatically flagged and removed from future sends — so you're never wasting sends on bad numbers.
7
Daily & weekly send limits
Thoughtful sending caps prevent over-messaging. Parents stay engaged — not annoyed — and your school's number stays trusted.
8
Two-way conversation signals
Parents are gently asked to reply with "Received". Even one reply per parent establishes a real conversation — keeping your messages in their main inbox.
9
Dedicated number per school
Each school has its own WhatsApp number and its own server. Your sends are never mixed with another school — fully isolated and protected.
10
Continuous delivery monitoring
Every send is tracked. Failure rates are monitored in real time. If anything looks off, your school is notified immediately on WhatsApp.

Result: 98%+ delivery rate, consistently, every school day.
